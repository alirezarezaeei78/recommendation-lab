What the scores mean

BPR scores a fitted user vector against learned item factors; it does not explain a score as a sum of liked-item neighbours. Item-kNN sums shrunk cosine similarities. Popularity counts positive training users. Scores are ranking signals, not probabilities, and have different scales.

The included catalog is authored synthetic demonstration data. BPR is fitted locally at startup with the declared seed13 default; it does not personalize an unknown user. Real benchmark results use a separate chronological research protocol. Unknown interactions are not known dislikes. No external APIs, sign-in or tracking.
