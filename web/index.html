What the scores mean

BPR scores a fitted user vector against learned item factors; it does not explain a score as a sum of liked-item neighbours. The centroid averages learned item vectors; it is not BPR user inference. Item-kNN sums shrunk cosine similarities. Popularity counts positive training users. Scores are ranking signals, not probabilities, and have different scales.

The included catalog is authored synthetic demonstration data. BPR is fitted locally at startup with the declared seed13 default; the new factor-centroid option scores a supplied history without a fitted user vector. Its three-like fallback threshold is a declared heuristic, not an optimized rule. Real benchmark results use a separate chronological research protocol. Unknown interactions are not known dislikes. No external APIs, sign-in or tracking.
