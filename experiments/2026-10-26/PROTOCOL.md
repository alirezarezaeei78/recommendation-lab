# Frozen sparse-history protocol - 26 October 2026

Frozen before implementing/evaluating the new policy. This is an exploratory query-history ablation on the already exposed 19/23 October MovieLens100K task, NOT an independent validation or a new-user holdout.

Question: how do ranking and coverage change when a query retains fewer known positive items, with the trained model and candidate set held fixed? Can an app expose an explicit sparse-history fallback and matching exports? Hypothesis: a fixed fallback may reduce dependence on sparse positive histories, but it may also lose useful personalized signals. No winner is assumed. Threshold is fixed prospectively at THREE unique positive catalog items; no sweep, tuning or business claim.

Reuse exact original global chronological cutoff at sorted timestamp position79999, including ties:80003 past rows,19997 future rows. Rating>=4 is positive. Full train catalog and full original training-rated exclusion are fixed for all methods/conditions. Primary95 eligible warm users, secondary192 eligible cold users, fourteen other future users excluded as before. Eligibility/relevance never change with truncation. Recall denominator is all eligible future positives. Lexical ties after score and train popularity. Report fixed-k query conditions0,1,3,5,10,all; retain min(k,available) most-recent positive items, timestamp descending then lexical ID. Keep all users, including histories shorter than k. Report actual retained supports and full-history bins0,1-2,3-4,5+ with NDCG/Recall/coverage/fallback counts.

Fixed training objects: reconstruct original train-only popularity and item-kNN(neighbors20,shrinkage10), reload exact23 October BPR item/user factors for seeds13/29/47, verify hashes and baseline metrics against immutable92a1c41. Do NOT retrain or select seeds. Training metadata and original data/split hashes remain. Full training interactions informed the frozen item factors/similarities, including interactions removed from each query. Full seen history remains for candidate exclusion. Thus this estimates query-history sensitivity of a frozen catalog model; it does NOT simulate full retraining after history loss, genuine new-user generalization or removal of those events from model training.

New factor-centroid heuristic: mean of learned item vectors for retained likes, score candidate by dot product with this mean; no fitted user vector is read. It is an authored heuristic over trained BPR item embeddings, NOT BPR user inference or a reproduction of the original paper. Empty history falls back to popularity. New history_auto: fewer than3 unique positive items -> train-only popularity; otherwise -> factor_centroid. Unknown user ID with no history returns explicit popularity; unknown user with supplied catalog history can use it; unknown item IDs remain validation errors. Known profiles and custom histories are mutually exclusive. All methods use identical candidates. Prior fitted-user BPR is reported ONLY as full-history reference, never as a sparse-query comparison because it retains a full user factor.
