# Frozen candidate-sampling comparison

Question: How do full-catalog versus sampled-candidate evaluation metrics differ when the trained recommender, queries and observed-relevance denominator stay fixed?

Freeze the existing three BPR factor archives from23October (seeds13,29,47), verified against results/2026-10-23.json. No fitting/tuning, no choice of best seed. Same chronological MovieLens100K task: cutoff889237269;80003past rows,19997strictly later rows, rating>=4observed relevance. Use training catalog1616items; exclude every past-rated item. Primary95warm eligible queries; secondary192cold queries; all287queries retained. BPR falls back to training positive-count popularity for unfitted users; report fallback counts. Known repeatedly exposed evaluation remains exploratory. Future observed positives are not an estimate of all true preference; unobserved is not negative.

Conditions: full eligible catalog; all eligible observed future positives plus a uniform without-replacement sample of up to20,100,500remaining unobserved candidates. Candidate selection is explicitly label-assisted offline evaluation, not a deployable candidate generator or a new train/test split. Every observed relevant item is retained; Recall denominator is the same relevant count and ideal binaryDCG uses min(10,relevant count) in every condition. Sampling removes distractors, so metric changes are not model improvements. Candidate-pool size/distribution changes even though relevance denominators match.

For sampling seeds101,202,303,404,505, create one per-user permutation of sorted eligible non-relevant items with NumPy default_rng(SeedSequence([sampling_seed, int(user_id)])). Prefix20/100/500 gives nested candidate pools. Reuse identical candidate sets across BPR seeds and popularity. Full condition once per model seed; sampled conditions all5seeds. No resampling after results. BPR score uses November4 fixed-order float64 accumulation over the same32factors; popularity train-only positive-user count. Sort by descending score, descending popularity, ascending ASCII itemID; top10.

Report every3modelseeds x(1full+3sizes*5samplingseeds)x2methods=96conditions, allwarm/cold/allcohorts, macro binaryNDCG@10 andRecall@10, candidate-count min/median/max, relevant supports, query/candidate hashes, fallback counts. Compare frozen full-catalog metrics with November4exact results to1e-12. Acrossseed summaries are descriptive mean/min/max, not confidence intervals; do not claim independence or production improvement. No latency claim. All negative/mixed results retained.
