What the scores mean

Item-kNN sums shrunk cosine similarities from your liked history. Popularity counts positive training users. Scores are ranking signals, not probabilities, and the two methods have different scales.

The included catalog is authored synthetic demonstration data. Real benchmark results use a separate chronological research protocol. Unknown interactions are not known dislikes. No external APIs, sign-in or tracking.
